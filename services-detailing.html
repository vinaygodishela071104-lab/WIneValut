WINE CELLAR DESIGN

Wine Cellar Design

Great wine spaces begin with thoughtful design. Our wine cellar design service transforms available space into an environment that balances storage capacity, architectural character and everyday usability. We consider the size and shape of the room, the collection it needs to accommodate, how the space connects with the surrounding interiors and the atmosphere you want to create.

Designed Around Your Collection

From intimate residential cellars to expansive tasting rooms, every layout is developed around the way your collection grows and how you want to experience it.

  • Collection-focused storage planning
  • Architectural layout development
  • Bottle capacity planning
  • Lighting and ambience planning
  • Material and finish selection
  • Tasting and display zones
  • Integration with existing interiors

CUSTOM WINE STORAGE

Custom Wine Storage

Every collection deserves storage that fits both the wine and the room. WineVault creates custom wine-racking systems that combine practical bottle organization with architectural detailing. Each storage solution is developed to suit the dimensions, capacity and visual character of the cellar.

Storage With Architectural Character

From traditional timber racking to contemporary display systems, every element is carefully considered to create a cohesive and enduring wine environment.

  • Custom timber wine racking
  • Wall-to-wall storage systems
  • Display bottle integration
  • Case and accessory storage
  • Label-forward presentation
  • Tasting display areas
  • Bespoke dimensions and configurations

CELLAR INSTALLATION

Cellar Installation

Precision installation brings the design from concept to reality. Our installation process coordinates structural preparation, wine racking, doors, glazing, lighting details, finishes and environmental systems to create a finished space that feels considered from every angle.

Precision From Start to Finish

Our team works carefully through every stage of installation, maintaining the intended design while ensuring each component is correctly positioned, finished and integrated.

  • Site preparation
  • Custom racking installation
  • Glass and door installation
  • Lighting integration
  • Finish coordination
  • Hardware installation
  • Final detailing and inspection

CLIMATE CONTROL

Wine Cellar Climate Control

Wine requires a stable environment to mature properly. Our climate-control solutions are planned around the specific requirements of the cellar, helping maintain consistent temperature and humidity while remaining discreetly integrated into the architecture.

Performance You Don't Have to See

The goal is a controlled environment that works quietly in the background while preserving the visual character of the room.

  • Temperature management
  • Humidity management
  • Cellar cooling systems
  • Air circulation planning
  • Insulation coordination
  • System integration
  • Environmental monitoring
Controlled Environment

Reimagine an Existing Space

Price factors

What changes the investment level?

The range is shaped by the scale of the room, preservation strategy, material palette, and level of customization.

Room size More square footage increases construction and system requirements.
Bottle capacity Higher capacity requires more structure, racking, and cooling.
Premium materials Specialty woods, stone, metals, and finishes add craftsmanship.
Ducted cooling Concealed systems require more planning and installation.
Smart monitoring Connected sensors, alerts, and controls add visibility.

Final pricing is prepared after reviewing the room dimensions, collection size, climate strategy, material preferences, and site requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity before the first pour

A few answers to the questions clients ask before planning a custom wine wall or cellar.

How much does a custom wine cellar cost?

The investment depends on room size, bottle capacity, climate control, construction, materials, glazing, and installation. Each project is priced according to its specific requirements.

Do you provide the climate-control system?

Yes. Climate strategy can include cooling equipment, humidity management, ventilation, ducting, electrical coordination, and monitoring.

Can you work with an existing room?

Yes. Existing rooms, under-stair spaces, basements, dining areas, corridors, and unused wall sections can all be assessed for conversion.

How many bottles can my space hold?

Capacity depends on the room dimensions, bottle formats, racking style, display requirements, and whether tasting or seating areas are included.
